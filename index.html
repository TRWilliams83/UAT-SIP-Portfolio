About Me

I’m a Digital Maker and Fabrication student focused on turning ideas into working prototypes through iterative design and testing. My work centers on CAD-driven development in Fusion 360, rapid prototyping with a Bambu P1P 3D printer, and building functional, self-sustaining systems.

Much of my recent work explores closed-loop environmental systems, where geometry and material behavior—capillary action, evaporation, and condensation—are treated as part of the engineering process. I approach projects like small experiments: design, build, observe what actually happens, then refine based on those results.

The goal is always to move beyond concept models and create functional prototypes that can be tested, improved, and eventually developed into a solid MVP.
