About Me

I am a Digital Maker and Fabrication student focused on turning ideas into working prototypes through iterative design, fabrication, testing, and revision. My work includes CAD development in Fusion 360, additive manufacturing with a Bambu P1P, mechanical systems, embedded electronics, and functional product development.

I approach projects as a process of identifying a problem, developing a practical solution, manufacturing a testable prototype, and using real results to improve the design. This process allows me to move beyond concept models and create products that can be evaluated for function, usability, manufacturability, and future development.

My Student Innovation Project, Project C2: Capillary Canopy, explores passive environmental control through 3D-printed terrarium lid geometry. My broader portfolio also includes mechanical assemblies, storage systems, ergonomic products, embedded electronics, and other prototypes completed throughout the Digital Maker and Fabrication program.
