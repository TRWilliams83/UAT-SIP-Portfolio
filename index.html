About Me

I am a dual-major student in Digital Maker and Fabrication and Robotics and Embedded Systems at the University of Advancing Technology. My work combines mechanical design, physical fabrication, electronics, programming, and testing to develop functional systems and prototypes.

I approach each project by identifying a practical problem, defining the minimum function the product must perform, creating a testable design, and using the results to guide the next revision. This process allows me to evaluate not only whether a concept works, but also how it can be manufactured, assembled, used, maintained, and expanded.

My portfolio includes Project C2: Capillary Canopy, Project Gaia, mechanical assemblies, additive-manufacturing projects, embedded monitoring systems, environmental sensors, custom enclosures, and product-development work completed throughout both degree programs.
